Privacy Policy

Studio Poster · Last updated: November 20, 2026

1. Summary

Studio Poster is a content studio run by Joao Rubens Marchete ("we", "us"). It helps creators make short videos and photo posts and send them to their own TikTok, Instagram and Facebook accounts. This policy explains what we access when a creator connects an account, how we use it, and how to have it deleted. We do not sell data or use it for advertising.

2. What we access

Only what a creator allows when they connect an account:

  • TikTok: basic profile information of the connected account (user ID, display name, username, profile picture), the account's posting settings (for example, which privacy levels are available), and permission to upload videos and photos to that account and, where the creator chooses, publish them (scopes user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish).
  • Instagram and Facebook: basic information of the connected Instagram professional account and Facebook Page, and permission to publish posts and Reels to them.
  • Access tokens issued by TikTok or Meta, which let the app post on the creator's behalf.
  • Content and captions that the creator provides or approves for posting, and a record of each post (date, platform, post ID, link).
  • Contact details (name, email) the creator gives us to work together.

We do not read followers, direct messages, comments, analytics or data about any other TikTok, Instagram or Facebook users.

3. How we use it

  • To upload and publish the creator's videos, photos and captions to the accounts they connected, as drafts or posts, as the creator has asked.
  • To show which account a post will go to and check posting settings before posting.
  • To keep a record of what was posted, so the creator and we can review it.
  • To contact the creator about their posts or this service.

We use each creator's data only for that creator's own posts.

4. Storage and security

Access tokens and post records are stored on our own computer, in files readable only by our user account, kept separately for each creator. They are not stored on a public server and are never posted online. Tokens are refreshed through the platforms' official APIs and are never shared with other creators.

5. Sharing

Data is sent only to:

  • TikTok and Meta (Instagram, Facebook), through their official APIs, to make the posts. Their own privacy policies apply to what they receive.
  • GitHub Pages (this website's host): for TikTok photo posts, the creator's images are placed on this website for a few minutes so TikTok can copy them, then removed.

We never sell data, share it with advertisers or other third parties, or use it to train AI models. We may disclose information if required by law.

6. Retention and deletion

We keep a creator's tokens only while they use Studio Poster. A creator can stop at any time:

  • Revoke access in TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions) or in Instagram and Facebook (Settings → Apps and websites). Revoked tokens stop working immediately.
  • Email us to disconnect. We delete that creator's tokens within 30 days, and their post records and contact details on request.

Posts already published stay on the platform until the creator deletes them there.

7. Your rights

Creators can ask to see, correct or delete the data we hold about them, or object to its use, by emailing the address below. We reply within 30 days.

8. Children

Studio Poster is for creators aged 18 or older. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly connect accounts belonging to children.

9. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version. We will tell connected creators about important changes by email.
